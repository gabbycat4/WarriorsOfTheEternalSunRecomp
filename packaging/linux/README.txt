Warriors of the Eternal Sun: Easy on the Eyes
=============================================
Unofficial native Linux port of Dungeons & Dragons: Warriors of the Eternal Sun
(Sega Genesis) with 240 Hz smooth motion, built on segagenesisrecomp.

Bring your own ROM: Warriors of the Eternal Sun (USA, Europe), CRC32 AF4A9CD7.
Nothing from the game is in this package. Choose the ROM in the launcher, or put
it into ~/.local/share/WarriorsOfTheEternalSunRecomp/.

Escape opens the menu. Hold B to dash on the world map and in town; on the
world map hold A + C for one second (standing still) to travel to the town.
